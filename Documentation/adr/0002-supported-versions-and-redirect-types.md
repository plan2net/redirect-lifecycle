# Support TYPO3 12 through 14 and normal redirects

TYPO3 12.4, 13.4, and 14 are mandatory target versions; TYPO3 11 is excluded. In TYPO3 14, automatic lifecycle management and existing redirect adoption apply only to normal redirects (`redirect_type=default`), excluding QR codes and short URLs because their lifecycle is outside the agreed scope. Exact supported patch versions and PHP combinations remain to be established and verified.
